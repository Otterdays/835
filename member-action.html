    Workplace issue / grievance preparation

    Article IV uses short calendar-day windows. Contact a steward promptly and confirm how “days” are counted for your case. Missed limits may stop a grievance unless the parties agree otherwise.

    1. Step 1 — Within 7 calendar days of the events, discuss with the applicable supervisor. Open Article IV
    2. Step 2 — If unresolved, reduce to writing within 7 calendar days of the Step 1 answer; employee + steward sign; submit to Assistant Project Manager.
    3. Step 3 — Appeal in writing within 7 calendar days of Step 2 to Director of Human Resources (or designee).
    4. Arbitration notice — If Step 3 is unacceptable, Union must notify Employer within 30 calendar days of the Step 3 answer.
