    First · Memorandum dated October 2, 2026

    2026–27 Tentative Agreement

    A proposed one-year extension to the 2022–26 CBA. The memorandum says the current agreement remains in place except for the changes described here.

    Tentative · outcome not shown
    What the supplied copy confirms

    The memorandum says the Union Committee would recommend the terms for ratification on or about October 5, 2026. Its signature and date lines appear blank. The supplied copy does not confirm whether it was signed or ratified.

    Term Pay & wages Pension & layoffs Discipline Overtime 2022–26 CBA
    1. 1

      Term · Article XXIV

      Proposed extension through June 30, 2027

      The memorandum proposes a term from July 1, 2026, through June 30, 2027, followed by the agreement’s year-to-year renewal and notice language. It also states the Employer is not bound if its Curran-Fromhold contract with the City is canceled or terminated.

      See memorandum page 1
    2. 2

      Pay · Article XXI

      Weekly wages and a 1.5% increase

      Wages are proposed to be paid weekly; UTO/STO accruals remain bi-weekly. The memorandum lists a 1.5% general wage increase effective July 1, 2026, with retroactive payment within 30 days of ratification in an employee’s regular paycheck.

      See memorandum page 1
    3. 3

      Pension & layoffs · Articles XX and V

      $2.65 per hour for pension; limited layoff language

      The proposed Employer pension contribution is $2.65 per hour worked, capped at 40 hours. The new layoff language says members will not be laid off to help pay for the July 2026 wage increase; it does not guarantee there will be no layoffs under unforeseen or emergency circumstances.

      See memorandum page 2
    4. 4

      Discipline · Article XXIII

      Discipline timing and badge-pulling language

      The proposal includes a ten-day discipline deadline with stated exceptions, addresses contact with the Prison about barring an employee, and says discipline under the Prison’s badge-pulling policy must be for just cause.

      See memorandum page 2
    5. 5

      Forced overtime · Article XVII

      Committee discussion and an attempt at notice

      The parties propose a labor/management meeting about forced overtime (“hooking”). If an employee is hooked, the Employer would attempt to give at least one hour’s notice unless notice is impracticable. The memorandum says nothing from that meeting is binding unless mutually agreed to in writing.

      See memorandum page 3
