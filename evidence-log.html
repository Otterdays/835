    Case Notebook

    Write down the facts of a workplace issue, one note at a time, so you are ready for a grievance, a steward meeting, or a conversation with your Business Agent.

    How it works

    1. Add a note for each thing that happened or each document you have: a schedule change, a written warning, a text from a supervisor.
    2. Pick how solid it is. Do you have proof, or did someone just tell you?
    3. Download your notes and bring them to your steward. Notes are kept only on this screen. Nothing is saved or sent to the union or anyone else, so download before you close the page.

    Leave out: inmate information, prison-security material, access codes, post orders, personal medical details, legal advice from a lawyer, or secret recordings. Keep your originals as they are and use these notes as a working copy.
