    November 20, 2026

    835–USF side letter for the DOC Hospital temporary project

    Project: The supplied Side Letter Agreement covers a temporary project at the Department of Corrections (DOC) Hospital. It was entered into November 12, 2026 and describes a project expected to run approximately two weeks, from on or about November 16 through on or about November 30, 2026.

    Staffing: The agreement lists one Electrician, one Painter, two Plumbers, and one Maintenance Trades Helper, staffed with bargaining unit volunteers. Assigned employees remain covered by the CBA and return to their regular facility, shift, and assignment when the project ends.

    Other terms: The parties will meet before a significant extension to address its effects. The Union reserves its right to demand bargaining if the project or work becomes permanent. The agreement is project-specific and includes a no-waiver clause.

    Signature status: The supplied copy has Bryant Barr's handwritten Union signature as Business Manager dated November 13, 2026. The U.S. Facilities signature block is blank.

    Roster: Six people previously supplied remain listed as temporary DOC assignments alongside their primary sites. The agreement's staffing counts are recorded separately; the source does not identify which named people fill each position.

    November 17, 2026

    Weekly pay transition discussed after negotiations

    Negotiations: The parties have finished negotiations with the company. As reported for members, the pay schedule is changing from bi-weekly to weekly. The effective date and transition schedule have not been provided.

    Management update: Susan Laramore-Johnson, identified as USF / USF HR, said:

    “We haven't established a timeline yet, as we first need to process the union increases. Once the timeline is in place, management will share all updates about the transition directly.”

    Current status: No implementation timeline is established. Management says it will share transition updates directly once a timeline is in place. This notice does not state a first weekly payday or other payroll dates.
