    Bargaining tools

    Separate verified facts from reports and open questions. This page is not legal advice and does not authorize a strike or unsafe action.

    Keep petition signature sheets offline and private. Do not collect employee numbers or home addresses on this site.

    Status snapshot (working)

    Verified (V)

    • Federal court monitor reports reference U.S. Facilities services at PDP facilities (public context only — not unit bargaining proposals).
    • Digitized CBA text and scans for the 2022–2026 term are available in this portal (complete holidays article still pending later pages).

    Reported (R)

    • Members report CBA expiration around July 1, 2026 — obtain signed duration clause / extension writing.
    • Members report an August 31 verbal deadline/extension — obtain signed writing or messages.
    • Members report wage and layoff package rumors — obtain written employer proposals.

    Open questions (Q)

    • Exact extension terms, if any.
    • PICC recognition / side-letter status relative to Article I.
    • Authorized vs budgeted vacancies vs contractual minimums.

    Legal review (L)

    • Consult Pennsylvania union-side labor counsel with the complete CBA, proposals, bylaws, and City-contract materials before relying on any strategy.

    Document request checklist

    • Complete signed CBA, wage schedules, appendices, side letters, amendments
    • Local bylaws / IUOE constitution provisions on bargaining and ratification
    • Any signed extension, ground rules, or written deadline messages
    • Every written proposal and counterproposal / tentative agreements
    • Staffing rosters, vacancy lists, overtime, contractor usage, layoff notices
    • Relevant U.S. Facilities–City of Philadelphia contract scopes and change orders
    • Grievances, settlements, and awards on staffing, subcontracting, layoffs

    Meeting-ready language

    We are asking for an informed, lawful, member-supported bargaining process. We are not accusing anyone of wrongdoing based on rumors, and we are not authorizing a strike or unsafe action. We want the documents, the exact proposal, a legal review, and enough time to understand the complete text before any vote.
